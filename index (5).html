Tuning drifts every time you play. It's the fastest thing you can fix and the biggest thing that makes practice sound wrong when your fingers are actually right.

The method, briefly

What the teaching consensus actually agrees on.

  • Short and daily beats long and rare. 15–30 focused minutes most days outperforms a weekend marathon.
  • Use easy chord shapes first. One- and two-finger versions let you make music now; add fingers later.
  • Drill the changes, not the chords. Switching is the bottleneck — see the Drill tab.
  • Half your time on songs. Motivation is a skill input, not a luxury.
  • Play to a beat from day one, and record yourself weekly to hear what you can't feel.
